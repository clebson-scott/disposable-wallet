# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Be precise, be kind, be honest. This is a small independent research repo; we treat contributors, issue reporters, and the people whose addresses we study with respect.

## Standards

**Expected:**
- Evidence over assertion — bring data, scripts, or sources.
- Declaring limitations of your own analysis (see `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` for ours).
- Plain language for non-experts.

**Unacceptable:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or doxxing of anyone, including addresses' owners.
- Presenting unverified claims as findings.
- Attack tooling or requests for it (see `SECURITY.md` for scope).

## Enforcement

Report to clebsoncamposdearaujo@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed by the maintainer. Contributions that violate this conduct may be reverted.
